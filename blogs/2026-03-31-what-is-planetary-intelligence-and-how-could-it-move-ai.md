By combining large-scale AI models with sensing capabilities, machines could help us better understand the world in real time. Known as "planetary intelligence," this could have significant implications for climate adaptation, economics, security and other global risks. At the World Economic Forum Annual Meeting 2026, participants will explore how the ethical use of AI could create solutions for real-world challenges.
